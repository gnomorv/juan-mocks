Stage requirements

Documents each stage needs before the deal moves on. The current stage is highlighted.

    Click a stage to open or close it. Link the SOW on the current stage to see "Ready to advance" appear.

    What this proposes and why

    The Stage requirements panel on a deal shows every sales stage as a stepper, not only the current one. Only the current stage can be changed, and only it decides whether the deal is ready to move on.

    1. All stages are shown, as a stepper. First Contact, Discovery and Proposal sit in order, with a line joining them. The rep sees where the deal has been and what comes next, not just today's checklist.
    2. The current stage is open and highlighted. It carries the brand tint and a filled marker. One stage is the one to work on; it should be the one that is already open.
    3. Past and future stages are closed, read-only, each with an x/n count. They open on click to show their rows, with no actions. The count says enough at a glance; the rows are there to check, not to edit.
    4. "+ Link" appears only on the current stage's unmet requirements. Linking a document to a past or future stage would not change what the deal needs now.
    5. "Ready to advance" reflects only the current stage. It appears in the panel header when the current stage reaches its full count (2/2 here). Advancing is gated on the current stage; a full past stage must not suggest the deal can move.

Overview

The Analytics Hub MVP is an analytics platform on a cloud analytics warehouse. It lets Globex Corporation bring subscription data from several sources together and act on it.

Goals

  • Consolidate subscription data from PayCo, AppMart and a second app store
  • Track and forecast revenue in real time
  • Self-service reporting for business stakeholders
  • Cut data latency from 24 hours to under 15 minutes

Technical architecture

Analytical queries run on the warehouse's SQL pools, a workflow scheduler orchestrates the ETL, and a BI tool draws the reports. Data sits in a dimensional model built for subscription analytics.

What this proposes and why

An early shell for Clara Studio: a client tree on the left, one header with the project's views, and Clara as a floating chat bubble that opens over whatever you are looking at. Switch the views in the header and open the bubble to see each part.

  1. Clara is a floating bubble on every view, not a page. Bottom right, with a soft pulse until you open it. Help is always one click away without leaving the work.
  2. The chat slides out over the current view. It knows the project you are in and keeps the view visible behind it. You can ask about what is on screen without copying it into a separate chat page.
  3. Tool use is shown inline. Here Clara reads a file and points at the line causing the error. Seeing what Clara read makes her answer checkable.
  4. One header carries the breadcrumb and the project's views. Projects, Artifacts, Dashboard and Settings. Where you are and what you can look at sit on the same line.
  5. The sidebar is a client tree. Client › product › project, with each project's lifecycle state: Active, Warranty or Planning. The state is visible before you open a project.
  6. Projects are cards with three numbers. Effort, work items and progress, or warranty days left once delivered. The numbers that matter change with the project's state.
  7. Artifacts are a list and a reader side by side. Filter by type, pick one, read it on the right. Moving between a spec and its estimate needs no page loads.
  8. The dashboard puts status, AI cost and recent activity together. One glance answers how the project is going and what Clara has been doing.

What this proposes and why

One search for the whole app: a Search control in the top bar that opens a search bar over any page, also on ⌘K (Ctrl K on Windows). It finds places by name and content in full, in one list grouped by kind.

  1. One search bar, from the top bar or ⌘K anywhere. The top bar shows the shortcut next to the word Search. Search is reachable from every page without leaving it, and the shortcut teaches itself.
  2. Workspaces and projects match by name, under "Jump to". Up to four workspaces and eight places in total, marked "by name". These are places to go, not text to read; matching their names is instant and predictable.
  3. Documents, issues and comments are searched in full. When the match is in the body, a one-line snippet shows it highlighted. People remember a phrase from a document or a comment more often than its title.
  4. Name matches show at once; the full search follows after a short wait. A spinner in the input marks the server search (about a quarter of a second after typing stops). Jumping to a known workspace stays instant, and the server is not queried on every keystroke.
  5. Choosing a result opens it. Here a note shows the route the app would open, such as /clients/acme-corp/issues/17. Every result is a destination, so the list never ends in a dead end.
  6. Keyboard first. Arrow keys move the highlight, Enter opens, Esc closes and returns focus to where it was. The people who use ⌘K expect to finish without the mouse.
